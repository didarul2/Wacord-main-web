# User Data Deletion Instructions — Wacord

**Last updated:** 2 August 2026  
**Product:** Wacord  

In accordance with Meta’s Platform Terms, this page explains how you can request complete deletion of your data associated with the Wacord Facebook / Instagram integration.

---

## How to request data deletion

You can request complete data deletion by following these steps:

### Step 1
Go to your Facebook **Settings & Privacy** → **Settings**.

### Step 2
Navigate to **Business Integrations** (or **Apps and Websites**).  
Direct link: [https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=business_tools](https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=business_tools)  
(Alternate: [Apps and Websites](https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=applications))

### Step 3
Find **Wacord** in the list and click **Remove** to revoke access.

### Step 4
Send an email to **[support@wacord.com](mailto:support@wacord.com)** with the subject line:

**Data Deletion Request**

Include your registered Wacord email address or Account ID.

### Step 5
Our support team will manually purge all your associated chat data, tokens, and records from our database **within 7 business days** and send a confirmation email.
